# Data conventions

All tables use UTF-8, one header row and stable identifiers. Present values describe their recorded publication or study assumption. Unknowns use blank, null, unknown, not_tested or explicit status according to their originating table. Absence of evidence does not act as a valid value. The catalogue publisher classification is used for discoverability and may cross subjects.

| Table group | Identifier | Semantics |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor catalogue | research ID / canonical URL | One indexed card per row, with same-title route candidates in alternate ledger; overlapping vendor tags, public metadata/source boundaries. See labster_catalog_data_dictionary.md. |
| AP curriculum | experiment_id | Unique proposed activity allocation with subject, class, resource/book version, printed/PDF page, method summary and product research fields. Combined table equals the union of three subject tables. |
| Deep dives | DD-C/P/Bxx | 12 official-public desk examples, four per subject, and categorical published-only/test status. Vendor catalogue documented examples are a separate supplementary comparison, with overlap by title. |
| Apparatus and reuse | apparatus/workstation IDs | Proposed asset geometry, provenance, contact and appearance requirements. Unknown dimensions remain pending. |
| Models | model_id + version | Admitted or proposed theoretical, synthetic or missing empirical status; explicit source and tests. No data generated by the tutor. |
| Performance | benchmark_id | OBSERVED control timing or PROPOSED target; BLOCKED vendor technical data. |
| Cost scenarios | registered count and low/base/high case | USD monthly unless metric indicates another unit; DAU, concurrency, sessions, data, cache, AI, exports, storage, DR, support and market-price date or budget assumption. |
| Traceability | REQ-ID | Owner/public need, source, documentation, architecture, epic and AT test. |
| Evidence sources | source_id | Unique identifier, public URI where available, as-of date, evidence class, note and package evidence location. |

Dates are ISO 8601. Engineering physics use SI internally with validated interchange units. Product evidence distinguishes terminal research QA from future software, wetted bench or device acceptance.

The concept crosswalk uses stable vendor and AP IDs to trace twelve provisional topic-level parallels. AP release evidence and original design are required independently.
